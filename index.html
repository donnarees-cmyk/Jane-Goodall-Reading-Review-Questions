📍 Why Jane Went to Gombe

Jane Goodall went to Gombe National Park to study chimpanzee behavior. She wanted to observe and understand how chimpanzees live, communicate, and interact.

🐾 Animals Jane Saw

Jane came across a buffalo, a leopard, and baboons. She did not see horses, kangaroos, or hippos.

🌄 The Peak

The Peak was one of Jane's favorite places. It was quiet and peaceful, and it had beautiful views that helped her think and write.

🐒 What Jane Learned About Chimpanzees

✅ True
• They use sounds to communicate.
• They live in groups.
• Young chimps stay with their mothers until about age 5 or 6.
❌ False
• They have one mate for life.
• They eat mostly small animals.
• They eagerly interact with humans.

💬 Vocabulary

vanished
disappeared, or can no longer be seen.
miserable
unhappy or uncomfortable.
impetuous
acting without thinking first.

👧 Jane Was Patient

Jane waited months for the chimpanzees to get used to her. When a chimp charged at her, she stayed calm and did not run. This helped her earn their trust, even when they were afraid.

🌿 A Visitor at Camp

When a large male chimpanzee came to Jane's camp, it showed that the chimpanzees were beginning to accept her.
